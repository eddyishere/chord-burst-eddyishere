遊戲規則 / Rules

  1. 按 START CAMERA，允許鏡頭。
  2. 左手選根音，伸出幾隻手指就代表哪個音：一隻係 C，兩隻係 D，三隻係 E，四隻係 F，五隻係 G。
    要出 A，伸出拇指同尾指，呢個就係「六」。
    要出 B，將拇指、食指同中指三個指尖合埋，呢個就係「七」。
  3. 右手選和弦屬性，一樣用手指數目：一隻係 Major，兩隻係 Minor，三隻係 Maj7，四隻係 m7，五隻係 Dom7。
    要出 Half-dim7，同樣伸出拇指同尾指，呢個就係「六」。
  4. 左右手一齊比出手勢並保持穩定，就會鎖定同持續播出和弦。
  5. 亦可點左右彩色按鈕，或用鍵盤：根音 1–7 或 A S D F G H J，屬性 Q W E R T Y，空白鍵重播。

沒有鏡頭？面板同鍵盤一樣可以玩。

  1. Press START CAMERA and allow the webcam.
  2. Left hand picks the root by how many fingers you hold up: one is C, two is D, three is E, four is F, five is G.
    For A, hold out your thumb and pinky. That is shape “6”.
    For B, pinch your thumb, index and middle fingertips together. That is shape “7”.
  3. Right hand picks the chord quality the same way: one is Major, two is Minor, three is Maj7, four is m7, five is Dom7.
    For Half-dim7, also hold out your thumb and pinky. That is shape “6”.
  4. Show both hand shapes at the same time and hold still to lock in and keep playing the chord.
  5. You can also tap the side buttons, or use the keyboard: roots 1–7 or A S D F G H J, qualities Q W E R T Y, Space to replay.

No camera? Panels and keyboard still work.
